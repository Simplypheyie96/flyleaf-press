## The role split

These are two complete look systems and cannot both be the direction — they would average into something
that reads as neither. So the archival refs govern **ground, card, colour, type**; the travel-journal refs
govern **attachments and chrome** only. Nothing from the travel-journal refs touches ground colour or card
corner radius. See `DESIGN.md` → Direction.
