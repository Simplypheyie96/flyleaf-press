Review app · type options · draft 01

Which hand writes the review,
and which face sets the page.

Two separate decisions. The top half is the face your actual review text is set in. Read it at length, not as a logo. The bottom half is the magazine layer: book title, author, and the small uppercase labels that carry rating, format and dates. Drag the slider if a face only works larger or smaller.

The hand

your review text · six faces

Kalam

Your pick

Upright, tidy, ballpoint-on-lined-paper. Holds up best of the six at long lengths. Worth knowing: Flyleaf already loads Kalam, so the two apps will share a voice.

I went in expecting a quiet book and got something with teeth. The first hundred pages are patient almost to a fault, and then the whole thing turns. Every detail I'd shrugged past comes back sharpened. I finished it on a Sunday and sat with it for an hour.

Caveat

Fastest hand

A quicker, slanted ballpoint with real personality. Sets small, so it needs a size bump: shown here at 22px against Kalam's 17px. Cleanest of the "fast" hands.

Shantell Sans

Most legible

Marker-drawn but built like a text face, with variable weight, a true italic, and the widest counters here. The safest choice for a review that runs to two pages.
